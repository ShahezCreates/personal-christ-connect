# Delhi NCR source notes

The active V5 UI is intentionally scoped to CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Delhi NCR Campus.

Official campus: https://ncr.christuniversity.in/
Official academics: https://ncr.christuniversity.in/academics
Official School of Sciences: https://ncr.christuniversity.in/departments/Delhi%20NCR%20Campus/School%20of%20Sciences/SCIENCES%20NCR
Official school page: https://ncr.christuniversity.in/schools/school-of-sciences
Official dining facilities: https://ncr.christuniversity.in/center/C/HD/dining-facilities
Official academic calendar 2026-27: https://ncr.christuniversity.in/view-pdf/academic-calendar-2026-2027
Official Delhi NCR social hub: https://linktr.ee/christdelhincr
Official gallery: https://ncr.christuniversity.in/gallery
Official campus map target / directions: Google Maps query is generated for the Delhi NCR campus address.

Current publicly indexed Delhi NCR school structure used by the app:
- School of Business and Management
- School of Commerce, Finance and Accountancy
- School of Humanities and Performing Arts
- School of Law
- School of Psychological Sciences
- School of Sciences
- School of Social Sciences

The public School of Sciences page identifies Computer Science, Mathematics and Statistics; other pages also surface current Delhi NCR departmental activity such as Economics, English & Cultural Studies and Psychology.

Current university-level / campus support bodies represented as separate entities rather than pretending they are all clubs:
USC, SWO, CAPS, CDL, CAI, CCHS, CIIC, NCC, Physical Education, OIA, CSA.

The active pages also use current public Delhi NCR activity names visible in the university feed, including PLACETRIALS 2026, TARANG 2026, ENNOVERSE 2.0, CARNIFEST 4.0, THE ART & SCIENCE OF CINEMATIC LIGHTING, CHANGE OF GUARD CEREMONY, FINALYTICS CLUB EVENT, STARTUP STORIES-MARKETING CLUB EVENT, and similar current listings.

Dining scope is based on the official Dining Facilities page: Gourmet Hall counters (Punjabi Bites, Southern Delights, Taste of Dilli, Rolls Lane, Bites & Brews), Rooftop Cafe counters (Steaming Mugs, Giani's Ice Cream), Domino's, Fresheteria and Cafe Coffee Day. Coffee vending machines / hostel vending machines are not treated as preorder stores because the official page does not publish them as full menu-order counters.

The active site does not claim an exhaustive verified list of every individual department-level club because the public university pages do not expose one canonical, current club roster. Activity-linked groups are marked accordingly instead of being presented as official exhaustive club membership records.

Photography URLs in the active app are Flickr-hosted images exposed through the university's Delhi NCR public gallery. They should be treated as external official-gallery assets, not as local project-owned photographs.
